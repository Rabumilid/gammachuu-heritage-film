# Design Document — The Story of Gammachuu

## 1. Project Overview

**Project:** Gammachuu Heritage Film  
**Working title:** The Story of Gammachuu — Determination and Success  
**Primary language:** Afaan Oromoo  
**Subtitle language:** English  
**Format:** Short AI-generated documentary-style film

## 2. Target Audience

The primary audience is Afaan Oromoo-speaking young people and adults, particularly those who may be facing financial, educational, or personal challenges.

The film is intended to preserve and communicate an inspiring life story while demonstrating how determination, patience, and learning can create opportunities across generations.

## 3. Audience and Distribution

The film will be designed for digital audiences and social media platforms. It will have both vertical (9:16) and horizontal (16:9) versions.

Afaan Oromoo narration will communicate the story, while English subtitles will make it accessible to a wider audience.

## 4. Creative Intent

The film should make viewers:

- Understand Gammachuu's journey from hardship to success.
- Feel the challenges and sacrifices he experienced.
- Appreciate the importance of education, determination, and perseverance.
- Feel inspired to make positive decisions about their own futures.

## 5. Emotional Direction

The intended emotional progression is:

1. Reflection and hardship.
2. Uncertainty and determination.
3. Sacrifice and perseverance.
4. Progress and achievement.
5. Hope and inspiration.

## 6. Narration and Sound

Afaan Oromoo narration will be generated using Addis AI. English subtitles will be prepared separately and synchronized with the final narration.

An original instrumental soundtrack will be added at a low volume beneath the narration.
